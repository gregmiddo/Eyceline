What's included

IT Asset Disposition

Retire desktops, laptops, servers and network gear the right way. Every item collected, tagged and accounted for.

Collection

We pick up from your site in Darwin and around the Territory, and log every item as it leaves.

Tagged and tracked

Each device gets its own barcode asset tag, tied to its make, model and serial number.

Decommissioning batches

Equipment is processed in batches, so a whole job can be followed from pickup to final sign-off.

Audit-ready reports

A clear report of every device collected and what happened to it, ready for your records and auditors.

Data erasure built in

Every storage device is wiped and certified before anything else happens. See our data erasure service.

Reuse first

Equipment that still has life in it is refurbished and resold, which can offset the cost of the job.

How it works

Four steps, fully documented

Collect & tag

We pick up your equipment and record every serial number and asset tag.

Erase

Drives are wiped with Blancco, overwriting every sector and verifying the result.

Certify

You receive an erasure certificate for each device, matched to your asset list.

Sign-off

A final summary of every device collected and erased, for your records and auditors.
